*Source: hooknloop.com.au + repo catalog (`newsletter/data/catalog.js`), industry set + logo assets (`hooknloop-image-assets.zip`).*

## 2. Who uses it
Eight real verticals (these are the film's "worlds"):
**Signage & displays · Clothing & garments · Automotive / caravan / RV · Schools & education · Medical & aged care · Upholstery & furniture · Construction & industrial · Warehousing & logistics.**
Plus DIY / home makers, upholsterers, costume, small manufacturers.

## 3. Why customers buy it
The connection that is **reversible and reusable**. Glue is permanent and messy; a knot is fiddly and fights you; a zip-tie is single-use. Hook-and-loop **holds firmly, releases cleanly, and holds again** — a hundred times. That is the whole reason the category exists, and it's the emotional key.

## 4. Functional benefits
Strong grip on fabric/wall/plastic/metal/wood · reusable · adjustable · residue-free (non-adhesive grades) · wash-durable · heat/UV/salt resistant (HD grades) · fire-retardant options · cut-to-length.

## 5. Emotional benefits
Quiet reliability. Adaptability without commitment. The freedom to change your mind, re-arrange, re-pack, re-fit — and still have it hold. It **forgives**.

## 6. Most visually interesting applications (for the camera)
- **Macro of the mechanism itself** — hooks catching loops (an entire cinematic universe, and the most AI-artifact-forgiving hero material).
- Heavy-duty **orange** strap cinching cargo on a ute at dawn.
- A **carer** adjusting adaptive clothing for someone with limited dexterity (tender, human, hard-to-fake ownable moment).
- A kid pressing a strip onto a school project; a tradie's cable run; an upholsterer's workbench; a caravan re-packed for the road.

## 7. Brand personality
Practical, quietly confident, warm, unpretentious, distinctly Australian. Trade-credible but human. Not corporate, not cute.

## 8. Strongest brand promise
> **"Made to connect."** — the thing that holds what matters, then lets go, then holds again.
