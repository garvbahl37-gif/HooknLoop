## ORIGINAL MASTER SPEC — 45 seconds (creative reference, superseded above)

**MOVEMENT I — INTRIGUE (0:00–0:08)**

`0:00` **[S01]** Black. A single **breath in.** Fade up on a slow, soft landscape we can't place — pale filaments, monumental, drifting. Another field enters from above. *(No logo. No text.)*

`0:02.5` **[S02]** EXTREME MACRO. A hooked fibre reaches, hesitates, and **takes** a loop. **PRESS — a low, woody click.** The whole frame tightens.

`0:04` **[S03]** MATCH CUT on the shape of the catch → a child's small hand closing into a parent's hand, kitchen window light behind. Held one beat.
> *VO: "We spend our whole lives…"*

`0:06` **[S04]** Close on hands: a strip of black HooknLoop pressed onto the back of a child's school project. **A soft RIP** as the backing peels.
> *VO: "…holding things together."*

**MOVEMENT II — EXPANSION (0:08–0:30)**

`0:08` **[S05]** MACRO bridge: thousands of loops compress and spring — the texture of the cut itself.

`0:10` **[S06]** UPHOLSTERER'S BENCH. Weathered hands stitch sew-on tape to a furniture panel. **Needle, fabric, room tone.**

`0:12.5` **[S07]** SIGNAGE WORKSHOP, morning. A dot of hook-and-loop set behind a display panel; the panel lifts into place. *(Piano enters — felt, single notes.)*
> *VO: "The temporary. The permanent."*

`0:14.5` **[S08]** CABLE RUN, construction fit-out. Reusable straps cinch a bundle. **RIP. PRESS. RIP.** — now on the beat.

`0:16.5` **[S09]** DAWN. A ute tray. An **ORANGE** heavy-duty strap is thrown over cargo and cinched hard. **A big, satisfying RIP.** *(The one flare.)*

`0:18.5` **[S10]** MACRO rhyme: hook draws loop taut — the same motion as the strap, at 1000×.

`0:20.5` **[S11]** AGED-CARE SUNROOM. A carer's hands gently fasten adaptive clothing for someone whose fingers can't. Unhurried. Tender. **Quiet; birds outside.**

`0:23` **[S12]** SCHOOL, mid-morning. Children press cards onto a display board of dots; a wall of small connections. Movement wipe as a child crosses frame.

`0:25` **[S13]** CARAVAN PARK, gold light. Gear re-packed and strapped for the road; a door closes.
> *VO: "The things we build —"*

`0:27.5` **[S14]** WAREHOUSE. Fast hands wrap double-sided tape around a bundle — **PRESS-PRESS-PRESS** — rhythm at its peak, then a held silence.

**MOVEMENT III — TURN + REVEAL (0:30–0:45)**

`0:30` **[S15]** THE RELEASE. Back in a home. Slowly, tenderly, a hand **peels** a strip away — a child's room being changed as they grow. **A long, soft RIP.** No tear. No residue.
> *VO: "…and the things we rebuild."*

`0:33` **[S16]** The same hand **presses** the strip back — somewhere new. **PRESS.** It holds.

`0:35` **[S17]** HERO. MACRO: a hook draws a loop tight. The camera doesn't cut — it pushes **through** the contact point, and the fibres resolve, unbroken, into the through-lines of a life: a cable run → a garment seam → a strap over a full ute → two hands, meeting.
> *VO: "Turns out the smallest connections hold the most."*

`0:41` **[S18]** Everything releases to warm white. **Silence.**

`0:42.5` **[S19]** END CARD. Two black strips enter from opposite edges, meet centre — **one clean PRESS.** The wordmark resolves: **HooknLoop** (blue, orange *n*). Below: **Made to connect.** Quieter: **HooknLoop.com.au**. Hold 2.5s. Cut to black.
> *VO: "HooknLoop. Made to connect."*

## Cutting rhythm (for the edit)
- 0–8s: **slow** (2–2.5s holds). Let the mystery breathe.
- 8–30s: **accelerating** (2s → 1.2s), locked to the rip/press percussion. J- and L-cuts: let the next world's sound arrive a beat early.
- 30–35s: **hold** — the release gets air (3s).
- 35–41s: the hero is **one unbroken push** — no cuts.
- 41–45s: **stillness**, then one press.
