# PHASE-8 PROMPT PACK — "The Things That Hold"
Per shot: **A** master still · **B** negative · **C** Magnific enhancement · **D** image-to-video · **E** camera motion · **F** continuity · **G** failure conditions.
Rule: describe **physical reality**, not style words. Product geometry/colour is sacred. One orange flare only (S09/S17/S19).

---

## SHARED STYLE DNA  *(prepend to every A prompt)*
> Cinema still, shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with a Cooke S8/i spherical prime, 24fps, warm Australian daylight, filmic contrast, rich but un-crushed blacks, gently controlled highlight rolloff, natural colour (NO teal-orange grade), fine 35mm grain, subtle halation on speculars only, gentle lens softness toward the frame edges, physically plausible contact shadows. Photographed, not rendered. Restrained, editorial, premium television-commercial cinematography. 16:9.

## SHARED NEGATIVE  *(append to every B)*
> plastic/waxy skin, over-smoothed AI faces, extra or malformed fingers, distorted hook-and-loop weave, invented buckles, legible fake logos or text, floating/duplicate objects, impossible shadows, HDR glow, oversaturation, teal-orange LUT, neon, excessive lens flare, motion-blur smear, glossy AI sheen, stock-photo model smiling to camera, watermark, deformed ears/teeth, plastic-looking product.

## PRODUCT LOCK  *(applies to every product frame)*
Matte woven black hook-and-loop (visible loops + hook field, NOT glossy/plastic), ~20–50 mm strip width. Heavy-duty strap = HooknLoop orange `#E87722`, ~50 mm wide, loop/buckle end. No third-party branding legible. Magnific creativity LOW on all product/macro shots.
