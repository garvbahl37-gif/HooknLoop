The only body copy left is for the case where tracking is not yet attached. * Five headline variants collapsed to three. Shopify distinguishes one item from several in the wording; a shopper does not need that spelled out when the items are listed directly below. * Dropped the Shop-app download buttons, the bundle and nested-line branches, and the duplicated "Visit our store" links.
