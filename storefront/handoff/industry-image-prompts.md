# Nano Banana / Gemini image prompts — industry pages

Each page needs **1 hero + 5 use-case images**. Save them to
`storefront/public/img/industries/` using the naming in that folder's README
(e.g. `clothing-garments-hero.jpg`, `clothing-garments-1.jpg` … `-5.jpg`) and
they appear automatically.

## How to use
Paste the **STYLE** block first, then the scene line for the image you want.
Ask for the aspect ratio noted (hero **4:3**, use-cases **16:10**). Say
**"no text, no watermark, no logos."**

### STYLE (prepend to every prompt)
> Photorealistic editorial lifestyle/product photograph, premium and clean. Warm natural lighting with a subtle orange accent; deep-navy and neutral tones consistent with an Australian industrial-supplier brand (navy #142548, orange #E87722). Real-world Australian setting, sharp focus, shallow depth of field, high detail, 8k. Hook-and-loop (Velcro-style) tape clearly visible where relevant. No text, no watermark, no logos.
